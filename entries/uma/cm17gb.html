Team Breakdown

I went with an extremely simple, standard team for this Champions Meeting as I was still learning the scenario and didn't have much time to prepare. This course has quite a high stamina requirement for most Umas, and Christmas Oguri Cap almost completely ignores that requirement - In addition to having an easy dirt aptitude.

I used Independent Training to build both of my debuffers, which saved me a lot of time and was surprisingly effective - I will be writing an individual blog article with more information about this in the future.

Agnes Tachyon
Summary

Very simple Dominator debuff build with 564 Escapades, Keen Eye, the four hesitant skills and Lone Wolf. My sparks were mainly for Dirt and Mile aptitude, in order to get at least Dirt D and Mile B aptitudes (For reusing in future CMs)

Grass Wonder
Summary

Very simple dominator debuff build with 564 Escapades, Keen Eye, the four hesitant skills and Lone Wolf. My sparks were mainly for Dirt and Medium aptitude, in order to get at least Dirt D and Medium A aptitudes.

Oguri Cap
Summary

I decided to inherit U=ma2 as a Festive Miracle trigger, and Red Shift/LP1211-M as a backup acceleration. Red Shift has a late trigger on this track - but it still has the most impact of all of the uniques I have access to. My power stat was a bit lower than I would have liked on this Ace, but I hit all of the gold skills I wanted - and I never got a stronger one.
